# Watson development notes

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) before changing Watson. It contains the repository's
product conventions, safety boundaries, and verification commands.

Also follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), including the company-neutral
content rules and required secret checks before commits and pushes.

Do not commit local credentials, databases, logs, browser profiles, generated
build output, private package-manager configuration, or agent-session notes.
